ABOUT YINZAOSUO

An independent print production studio, run personally, from enquiry to shipment.

YINZAOSUO coordinates wide-format print production in China for print businesses overseas — one person, one point of contact, and your order followed through at every step.

THE STUDIO

Small on purpose. Direct by design.

YINZAOSUO is the trading name of an independent print production studio run by Wang Chi in China. It is a one-person studio, not a factory and not a trading company with a sales team — and this page would rather say that plainly than dress it up.

Production itself runs through partner production facilities in China that I work with order by order. My job sits between you and those facilities: I confirm the right material and specifications for your job, check your files before anything is printed, follow production, check the finished goods and packing myself, and arrange the export shipment. When you write to YINZAOSUO, you write to the person who will actually handle your order.

For trade buyers, that structure has a practical upside: nothing is handed off between departments, specifications are confirmed in writing before production, and if something needs attention, you already know exactly who to ask.

How I work is deliberately simple, and it is the same on a small order as on a large one. Every specification that matters — material, dimensions, quantity, finishing and packing — is confirmed with you in writing before production starts, and I follow the order personally while it is being produced with the partner facility matched to the job. Nothing is dispatched on trust: before anything leaves, the finished goods and their export packing are checked and photographed, and those photos go to you for confirmation first.

Freight is coordinated the same way — arranged per order, around what you have ordered and where it is going, with the transit timing and delivery terms stated in your quotation rather than promised in general terms here. Because the studio is one person, you are never handed from a salesperson to a production desk to a shipping desk: the person who quoted your order is the person who checks it, photographs it and arranges its shipment, and the one you write to if anything needs attention.

HOW AN ORDER FLOWS

From enquiry to shipment, step by step.

01

Enquiry

Tell me the product, dimensions, quantity and destination. The more specific the brief, the more accurate the quotation.

02

File check

Your artwork is checked personally before production, and anything that could affect the result is flagged back to you first.

03

Quotation & confirmation

Material, specifications, packing and freight are confirmed in writing, so both sides know exactly what has been agreed.

04

Production

Your order is produced with the partner facility matched to the job, and I follow its progress through production.

05

QC photos

Before dispatch, you receive photos of the finished goods and the packing, so you can see what is leaving and how it is protected.

06

Export packing & shipment

Goods are packed for export and the shipment is arranged as confirmed in your order, with the shipping documents prepared for your side.
