NEED TO KNOW

Questions?
We have thought ahead.

If you cannot find an answer, tell us about your project. We usually reply within one business day.

Talk to our team ↗
01Can I confirm material and production specifications before ordering?+

Tell us the application and the specifications you need to confirm, and we will confirm the available options before you commit. Material samples or a printed proof can be arranged for most media — ask when you request your quotation.

02Can you help if I do not have a design?+

Yes, design assistance is available. Prepare your text, images, and brand assets; we will discuss the requirements before quoting.

03What payment and delivery methods are available?+

Payment is by bank transfer (T/T) against a proforma invoice, so the specification, quantity and total are agreed in writing before you pay. Production time is confirmed in your quotation; sea freight to main Australian ports typically takes around 40 days in transit. Delivery terms and any import charges for your destination are stated in the quotation before you confirm.

04What if there is a problem with the print?+

Files and specifications are confirmed before production, and photos of the finished goods and their export packing are shared with you before dispatch. If a production quality issue still affects use, tell us with photos and order details — we will prioritize reprinting or agree on another appropriate solution.

05Can I combine different products in one order or shipment?+

Yes — different media and finished graphics can usually be combined into a single order, and where it makes sense they can be packed and shipped together. Send the full list with dimensions and quantities when you enquire; the combination, packing and freight for the combined shipment are confirmed in your quotation before you commit.

06How does ordering work, from enquiry to shipment?+

In six steps: (1) send your enquiry with the product, dimensions, quantity and destination; (2) you receive a quotation and proforma invoice with the specifications, packing and freight confirmed in writing; (3) payment is made by bank transfer (T/T) against the proforma invoice; (4) production runs once files and specifications are confirmed; (5) before dispatch, you receive photos of the finished goods and their export packing for your confirmation; (6) the shipment is then arranged on the terms confirmed in your quotation.

07What do you need from my artwork files?+

Send the artwork you have — every file is checked before production, and anything that could affect the printed result is flagged back to you first. Basic file checks and simple adjustments are included; larger revisions or original design work are quoted separately before any work starts, so there are no surprises in the total.
