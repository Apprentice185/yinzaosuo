TRADE TERMS

FOB, CIF or DDP — your choice.

We work with the trade terms that suit how you buy. Whichever you choose, the full cost is confirmed in writing in your quotation before production — no surprises added later.

FOB

You run the freight

We deliver your goods to the departure port in China and handle export clearance; your own forwarder arranges the main carriage.

CIF

We ship to your port

We arrange and pay the freight and insurance to your destination port; you handle import clearance and duties on arrival.

DDP

One landed price, door to door

We handle everything — freight, customs clearance and import duties — and deliver to your door for one confirmed landed price. Even on DDP terms, our core media typically lands around 40% below local trade prices: the saving survives the freight.

TRANSIT TIMES & TERMS

No generic freight promises. Your quotation is the promise.

Freight depends on what you order, how much of it, and where it is going — so it is arranged and priced per order rather than read off a rate card. As a reference point, sea freight to main Australian ports typically takes around 40 days in transit; production time is confirmed separately in your quotation. Air freight can be arranged for urgent orders and is quoted separately, as it prices very differently from sea freight.

PACKED FOR THE JOURNEY

Packed like it matters — because it does.

01

Rolls travel as rolls

Banner, mesh, vinyl, photo paper and films ship in roll form — compact and weight-efficient, the most economical way to move wide-format media by sea.

02

Boards packed flat & protected

Board products are packed flat, with protection against bending and corner damage for the journey.

03

Waterproof goods, kept that way

Every media line we export is waterproof and moisture-proof by nature — and packed to stay that way, with moisture protection inside the pack for the sea journey.

04

Checked before it closes

The packing itself is photographed as part of your pre-dispatch confirmation, so you can see exactly how your goods were packed.
