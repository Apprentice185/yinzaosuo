  A clear enquiry helps the supplier check the right material and prepare a more useful quotation. Before requesting pricing for large-format print media, collect the following details.

  ## 1. Product type

  Identify the exact material: gloss photo paper, satin photo paper, PP synthetic paper, self-adhesive vinyl or outdoor PVC printing film. If you are comparing options, explain the intended application rather than asking for a generic “banner material”.

  ## 2. Roll width and roll length

  The current core range includes 1520 mm-wide photo paper, PP synthetic paper and self-adhesive vinyl, plus 1270 mm-wide outdoor PVC printing film. Each is listed in 50 m rolls. Confirm whether you need full rolls, multiple rolls or cut sheets.

  ## 3. Media weight and liner details

  The confirmed media weights are 200 gsm for gloss and satin photo paper, 180 gsm for PP synthetic paper and 240 gsm for the PVC printing film. For self-adhesive vinyl, 120 gsm and 140 gsm refer to the release liner options, not the printable face film. Do not use liner gsm as a substitute for face-film thickness.

  ## 4. Quantity and order value

  State the number of rolls and/or total area in square metres. The current minimum order is 100 m², or an order goods value above USD 2,000; either condition is sufficient. If you are unsure how much material is required, provide the finished dimensions and expected number of prints.

  ## 5. Ink option

  Epson-compatible replacement ink is the standard option. Original Epson ink is available at an additional USD 5/m². State your preferred ink option when requesting a quotation.

  ## 6. Lamination and finish

  Standard finishing uses 100 gsm PVC cold-lamination film with a yellow release liner. Gloss and matte finishes are available. If your project has a particular installation or exposure requirement, describe it so the combination can be checked before production.

  ## 7. Packing method

  Standard roll packing includes a rigid paper tube inside the roll. Carton packing can be discussed as an additional option. If you need individual cartons, reinforced outer packaging or pallet handling, state this before the quotation is finalized.

  ## 8. Destination and delivery details

  Provide the destination country, city and postcode, plus any delivery constraints. Freight, duties and final delivery timing depend on the route and shipping arrangement, so they should be confirmed in the quotation rather than assumed from a general estimate.

一份清晰的询价信息，可以帮助供应方确认材料规格并准备更准确的报价。批量采购大幅面喷绘卷材前，建议先整理以下信息。

## 1. 产品类型

先明确具体材料：光面写真相纸、绸面写真相纸、PP合成纸、自粘背胶或户外PVC喷绘膜。如果还在比较材料，建议说明实际使用场景，不要只笼统地询问“广告喷绘材料”。

## 2. 卷宽与卷长

当前核心产品中，写真相纸、PP合成纸和自粘背胶的卷宽为1520mm，户外PVC喷绘膜的卷宽为1270mm；各产品当前列示的卷长均为50米。还需要确认采购整卷、多卷，还是需要裁切成单张。

## 3. 材料克重与底纸信息

光面和绸面写真相纸为200gsm，PP合成纸为180gsm，PVC喷绘膜为240gsm。自粘背胶的120gsm和140gsm指背纸/离型纸选项，不是可打印面膜的克重或厚度。不要用底纸克重代替面膜厚度。

## 4. 数量与订单货值

说明采购卷数和/或总面积（平方米）。当前最低起订条件为100平方米，或订单货值超过USD 2,000，满足其中一项即可。如果暂时无法确定用量，可以提供成品尺寸和预计画面数量，协助核算材料需求。

## 5. 油墨选项

标准配置为爱普生兼容替代墨水；可选爱普生原装墨水，价格增加USD 5/m²。询价时请明确希望采用哪种油墨。

## 6. 覆膜与表面效果

标准覆膜为100gsm PVC冷裱膜，带黄色离型纸，可选亮膜或哑膜。如果项目对安装方式或使用环境有特殊要求，请在询价时说明，以便提前核对材料组合是否适用。

## 7. 包装方式

标准卷装内置硬质纸筒。可以另行讨论纸箱包装。如果需要单卷纸箱、加强外包装或托盘装运，请在最终确认报价前提出。

## 8. 目的地与配送信息

提供目的国家、城市和邮政编码，以及收货地址方面的特殊要求。运费、税费和最终运输时间会随航线及运输安排变化，应在报价中按实际目的地确认，不宜直接套用一般估算。
