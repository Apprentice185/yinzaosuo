  When comparing self-adhesive vinyl, it is important to identify which layer a weight specification describes. In this product range, 120 gsm and 140 gsm refer to the release liner (the backing paper), not the thickness or gsm of the printable face film.

  ## What is the release liner?

  The release liner is the backing layer that protects the adhesive before application. It is removed when the printed graphic is installed. Its paper weight is one part of the product specification, but it does not tell you the thickness of the printable PVC face film.

  ## The listed 120 gsm and 140 gsm options

  The current self-adhesive vinyl range is supplied in 1520 mm width and 50 m rolls. Two release-liner options are listed:

  - 120 gsm release liner.
  - 140 gsm release liner.

  Pricing for both options is confirmed per quotation.

  The adhesive colour, adhesive chemistry, permanence and printable face-film thickness have not yet been confirmed. The liner weight alone should not be treated as proof that one option has a thicker face film, stronger adhesive or a longer service life.

  ## What should you confirm before ordering?

  Ask the supplier to confirm the printable face-film thickness, adhesive type, whether the adhesive is permanent or removable, the suitable application surfaces and any relevant indoor or outdoor-use limits. These details are not inferred from the 120 gsm or 140 gsm liner weight.

  Also confirm the finished graphic size, roll quantity, ink system, lamination and packing method. 比较自粘背胶时，首先要弄清楚规格中的克重描述的是哪一层。当前产品中的120gsm和140gsm指的是背纸/离型纸克重，不是可打印PVC面膜的厚度或克重。

## 什么是背纸/离型纸？

背纸是覆盖在胶黏层背面的保护层，在画面安装时会被揭除。背纸克重是产品规格的一部分，但它不能直接说明可打印PVC面膜有多厚。

## 当前列示的120gsm与140gsm选项

目前自粘背胶的卷宽为1520mm，每卷50米，提供两种背纸选项：

- 120gsm背纸。
- 140gsm背纸。

两种选项的价格均按询价确认。

目前尚未确认胶水颜色、胶黏剂类型、永久性或可移除属性，以及可打印面膜厚度。不能仅凭背纸克重，就判断其中一种面膜更厚、胶黏力更强或使用寿命更长。

## 下单前还应该确认什么？

建议向供应方确认可打印面膜厚度、胶黏剂类型、胶水是否永久型或可移除型、适用粘贴表面，以及室内或户外使用限制。这些信息不能从120gsm或140gsm底纸克重推断出来。

同时确认画面成品尺寸、采购卷数、油墨系统、覆膜和包装方式。当前标准配置为爱普生兼容替代墨水和100gsm PVC冷裱膜（黄色离型纸）；也可选择爱普生原装墨水；覆膜可选亮膜或哑膜，价格按询价确认。

## 一段可直接使用的询价示例

“请报价自粘背胶，卷宽1520mm，每卷50米，选择120gsm底纸。请确认胶水颜色、胶黏剂类型、面膜厚度，以及胶水属于永久型还是可移除型。画面将粘贴在[填写具体表面和使用环境]。请同时说明可选覆膜和包装方式。”

提供实际用途，并主动确认尚未明确的技术参数，可以降低材料与最终安装表面不匹配的风险。
