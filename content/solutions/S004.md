  ## Start with the booth plan and the show schedule

  Exhibition graphics are planned against fixed constraints: the booth footprint and wall heights set by the organiser, what the stand structure allows you to fix to, the build-up and dismantling windows, and how the graphics will travel to the venue. List the pieces the stand needs — back wall, dimensional lettering, product panels, information boards, counter fronts — and note which must survive multiple shows and which are one-event items. That split drives the material choice more than the artwork does.

  ## Divide the work between PVC foam board and KT board

  The two rigid boards on this site cover different ends of that split:

  - [PVC foam board](./products/ys-pvc-foam-board/) is a solid expanded PVC sheet. It takes direct printing, cuts cleanly into letters and shapes, and holds an edge — the usual choice for dimensional logos, shaped feature panels, and boards that will be re-used across shows.
  - [KT board](./products/ys-kt-board/) is a foam core faced with paper on both sides, printed and mounted as finished posters and panels. It is light and easy to handle and transport, which suits information boards, price and product cards, and graphics produced for a single event.
  - A typical stand mixes both: foam board where the piece is handled, cut, or kept; KT board where the piece is large, flat, and changed every show.

  Thickness, sheet size, and finishing for each piece are confirmed against the stand design in the project quotation — there is no standard set that fits every booth.

  ## Check details before production

  Work from the organiser's stand plan and confirm every finished size against it, including wall panel divisions so artwork breaks where the structure does. Agree how each piece is fixed (and what the venue allows), how boards are packed flat for transport, and which pieces need protective facing because they will be re-used. If graphics ship internationally to the show, build the freight time into the schedule — for Australian venues, sea freight to main Australian ports typically takes around 40 days in transit.

  ## Planning focus

  Exhibition graphics fail most often on fit and logistics, not on print. Assign rigid, re-usable pieces to PVC foam board and light, per-event pieces to KT board, confirm sizes against the stand plan, and the set arrives as one kit that builds quickly and packs down flat.

## 先看展位图和展期安排

展览画面的规划要围着几个固定约束转：主办方给定的展位面积与墙高、展架结构允许怎样固定、布展与撤展的时间窗口，以及画面如何运到场馆。先把展位需要的部件列出来——背景墙、立体字、产品展板、信息牌、柜台画面——并标注哪些要跨多场展会重复使用、哪些是一次性物料。这个划分比画面本身更决定材料怎么选。

## 按刚性需求在发泡板与 KT 板之间分工

本站的两种硬质板材正好覆盖这个分工的两端：

- [PVC 发泡板](./products/ys-pvc-foam-board/)是整张发泡 PVC 板，可直接印刷，切割成字形和造型后边缘整齐、挺括，适合做立体标志、造型展板，以及需要重复使用的部件。
- [KT 板](./products/ys-kt-board/)是两面覆纸的泡沫芯板，画面裱贴成成品海报与展板，轻、易搬运，适合信息牌、产品与价格卡，以及每场展会更换的画面。
- 常见的展位组合是两者混用：要动刀切割、要留着下次用的部件用发泡板；面积大、平面型、每场都换的画面用 KT 板。

每件部件的厚度、板幅与后道处理，应结合展位设计在项目报价中确认——没有一套尺寸能套所有展位。

## 制作前核对

以主办方的展位图为准逐件核对成品尺寸，包括墙板的分缝位置，让画面在结构接缝处断开。再确认每件的固定方式（以及场馆允许的方式）、板材如何平放打包运输，以及重复使用的部件是否需要加保护面。

## 规划重点

展览画面出问题，多半出在尺寸与物流，而不是印刷。把刚性、重复使用的部件交给发泡板，把轻量、单次使用的画面交给 KT 板，再按展位图逐件核对尺寸，整套物料就能快速布展、平整打包带走。
