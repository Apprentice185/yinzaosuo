  ## Start with the fence line, not the artwork

  Walk the full fence run first. Note which sections sit on temporary mesh fence panels and which are fixed hoarding, how exposed each stretch is to wind, where the entrances, gates, and pedestrian paths are, and what each section has to do — dust and privacy screening, safety messages, wayfinding, or project branding. A single fence line usually needs more than one material, and the split is decided by the site, not by the design.

  ## Combine mesh and solid banner by section

  As a general rule in wide-format work:

  - [Printed PVC mesh](./products/ys-pvc-mesh/) suits long, exposed runs. Its perforated surface lets part of the wind through, which reduces the load on both the print and the fence behind it, while still carrying large graphics and text.
  - [Solid PVC banner](./products/ys-pvc-banner/) suits sheltered sections, site entrances, and hoarding where full opacity matters — privacy screening, presentation graphics of the finished project, and contractor or developer branding.
  - Safety and access information usually works best as separate, clearly bounded panels at gates and crossings, rather than being stretched across a branding run.

  Which sections get which material, and the mesh or banner specification for each, are confirmed against your fence type and site conditions in the project quotation. For a deeper look at the mesh side of this choice, see the [PVC mesh fencing guide](./articles/pvc-mesh-construction-fencing-guide/).

  ## Check details before production

  Measure the run in sections rather than as one total length: panel widths, fence heights, corner turns, and gate openings all affect how artwork is split. Confirm how graphics repeat or continue across panel joins, where eyelets and fixing points should fall, and whether any sections need to be replaceable on their own (for example, panels carrying dates or stage information). For Australian sites, plan the order backwards from the installation date — sea freight to main Australian ports typically takes around 40 days in transit, on top of production time confirmed in the quotation.

  ## Planning focus

  Fence graphics work when the material follows the fence: mesh where wind and length dominate, solid banner where privacy and presentation dominate, and clear safety panels where people enter. Confirm the section plan, dimensions, and fixing method before files go to production, and the graphics will go up as one coherent run instead of a patchwork.

## 先看围挡，而不是先看画面

先把整段围挡走一遍再谈设计：哪些段是临时网片围栏、哪些是固定围板；每一段的迎风情况如何；出入口、大门和人行通道在哪里；每一段分别承担什么任务——防尘遮挡、安全提示、导视，还是项目形象展示。一段围挡通常不止用一种材料，分段依据是现场，不是画面。

## 按段落组合网布与实心横幅

行业里通行的组合思路是：

- [PVC 网布](./products/ys-pvc-mesh/)适合长距离、迎风的段落。网孔能让一部分风穿过，减轻画面和围栏本身承受的风压，同时仍可承载大幅画面与文字。
- [实心 PVC 横幅](./products/ys-pvc-banner/)适合背风段、出入口和需要完全遮挡的围板段，用于隐私遮挡、项目效果展示，以及开发商与施工方的品牌呈现。
- 安全与出入信息建议做成独立、边界清晰的牌面，放在大门和通道口，不要拉长融进品牌画面段里。

每段具体用哪种材料、网布与横幅的规格如何选，应结合围栏类型与现场条件在项目报价中确认。网布一侧的选材细节可参阅 [PVC 网布围挡选材指南](./articles/pvc-mesh-construction-fencing-guide/)。

## 制作前核对

尺寸按段量，不要只量一个总长：网片宽度、围挡高度、转角和大门开口都会影响画面如何分幅。再确认画面在拼接处如何重复或延续、扣眼与固定点的位置，以及哪些段落需要能单独更换（例如带日期或阶段信息的牌面）。

## 规划重点

围挡画面的关键是让材料跟着围挡走：迎风和长距离段用网布，需要遮挡和展示的段用实心横幅，人流出入处用清晰的安全牌面。制作前把分段方案、尺寸和固定方式确认好，整段围挡装出来才是一个整体，而不是东拼西凑。
