  ## Start with the surfaces, not the design

  Glass and vehicle work both fail on the same thing: artwork planned flat, applied to a surface that is not. Begin by listing every surface in the job — shopfront panes, office partitions, vehicle sides, rear, and bonnet — and note for each one whether it is flat or curved, what sits on it (handles, wipers, sensors, mullions), and what it has to do: privacy, identification, or full advertising. The artwork is then built per surface, so nothing important lands on a curve or a door handle.

  ## Plan glass and vehicles as one adhesive programme

  [Self-adhesive vinyl](./products/ys-adhesive-vinyl/) covers both settings, in a few standard roles:

  - On glass: frosted-look privacy bands at eye level for offices and meeting rooms, cut lettering for names and hours, and full-coverage prints where a pane should carry an image or block a view entirely.
  - On vehicles: cut lettering and logos for simple identification, printed partial panels (rear doors, side sections) for most trade fleets, and full wraps where the vehicle itself is the advertisement.
  - On rear and side windows, [one-way vision film](./products/ys-one-way-vision/) can extend the graphic across the glass while keeping outward visibility for the driver, subject to local rules on window coverage.

  As general material knowledge, flatter and shorter-term work is usually served by standard monomeric films, while curved panels and longer outdoor service point to polymeric films that conform and hold up better; the film grade for each surface is confirmed in the project quotation.

  ## Check details before production

  Measure each pane and each vehicle panel separately, and photograph the actual surfaces — the same vehicle model can differ by year and trim. Mark keep-clear zones in the artwork: window areas the driver needs, handles, badges, fuel flaps, and tight curves where text should not sit. Confirm the surface condition and cleaning preparation, since adhesion assumes clean, sound paint or glass, and agree on an application plan for large pieces so panels align across doors and seams.

  ## Planning focus

  Treat glass and vehicles as one family of adhesive work: decide per surface whether it needs privacy, identification, or advertising, keep key information off curves and hardware, and confirm measurements on the real surfaces. Do that, and a shopfront and a fleet come out reading as one brand.

## 先看贴面，而不是先看设计

玻璃贴和车身贴最容易出问题的地方相同：稿子是按平面做的，实际贴面却不是平面。先把项目里所有贴面列出来——橱窗玻璃、办公室隔断、车身侧面、车尾、引擎盖——逐面标注是平面还是曲面、上面有什么（把手、雨刮、传感器、竖框），以及这一面要承担什么：隐私、标识，还是整幅广告。稿面按贴面逐面拆，重要信息就不会落到曲面或门把手上。

## 玻璃与车身按一套背胶逻辑规划

[自粘背胶](./products/ys-adhesive-vinyl/)在两个场景里是同一套用法，常见的分工是：

- 玻璃上：视线高度的磨砂效果隐私带（办公室、会议室常用）、名称与营业时间的刻字贴、以及需要整幅画面或完全遮挡视线的整面印刷贴。
- 车身上：只做标识的刻字与标志、覆盖局部板面（后门、侧围）的印刷贴——这是大多数商用车队的选择、以及把整车当广告面的整车包覆。
- 后窗与侧窗可用[单透膜](./products/ys-one-way-vision/)把画面延伸到玻璃上，同时保留驾驶员向外的视线，具体要看当地对车窗覆盖的规定。

从材料常识看，平面、短期使用一般用普通单体膜即可；曲面板面和更长的户外使用周期更适合延展与耐候更好的高分子膜。每个贴面用哪一级膜，在项目报价中确认。

## 制作前核对

每面玻璃、每块车身板面单独测量，并给实际贴面拍照——同型号车辆不同年份和配置的外观也可能不同。在稿面里标出禁放区：驾驶员需要的视线玻璃区、把手、车标、油箱盖，以及不该放文字的急曲面。再确认贴面状况与清洁处理（粘合的前提是漆面或玻璃干净完好），大面积贴面要先约好施工对位方式，让画面跨车门与接缝时能对齐。

## 规划重点

把玻璃和车身当作同一类背胶工作来规划：逐面决定它需要隐私、标识还是广告，关键信息避开曲面和五金件，尺寸以实际贴面为准。这样做出来，门店玻璃和车队车身看起来会是同一个品牌。
