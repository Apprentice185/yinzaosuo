  ## Start with what the window has to do

  A shop window usually carries three jobs at once: attract people passing by, show the products or offers that matter this season, and keep the interior usable — daylight in, and staff able to see out. Note the window layout (fixed panes, doors, mullions), how much of the glass faces direct sun, and what sits directly behind the glass, because those factors decide how much of the window should be covered and with what.

  ## Build the window around one-way vision film

  [One-way vision film](./products/ys-one-way-vision/) is a perforated self-adhesive film: from outside it reads as one continuous printed image, while from inside the perforations keep outward visibility and let daylight through. It suits the large panes that carry campaign imagery. Two behaviours are worth planning around:

  - The effect depends on light balance. By day the brighter exterior makes the print read clearly; at night, with the interior lit and the street dark, the effect weakens and the interior becomes more visible — plan after-dark expectations accordingly.
  - Perforation patterns trade print density against see-through clarity: more open area sees through better, more printable area carries stronger colour. The right balance depends on viewing distance and how much the interior needs to see out, and is confirmed per project.

  Elements that must stay crisp at close range — logos, prices, opening hours — are usually better placed on [solid self-adhesive vinyl](./products/ys-adhesive-vinyl/) panels or cut lettering alongside the film, rather than in the perforated area itself.

  ## Check details before production

  Measure every pane separately, including the frame and mullion widths, and mark which panes are doors or openers. Confirm whether coverage is full-height or a band, how artwork aligns across mullions, and whether the shopping centre or lease sets rules on window coverage. Check the glass condition and agree on surface preparation, since film performance depends heavily on clean, sound glass.

  ## Planning focus

  A good retail window is planned as a light problem as much as a graphics problem: one-way vision film carries the campaign while keeping the shop bright and outward-looking, and solid vinyl carries the fine detail. Confirm panes, coverage, and artwork alignment before production, and seasonal changeovers become a film swap rather than a refit.

## 先明确橱窗要承担什么

店铺橱窗通常同时做三件事：吸引路人注意、展示当季的主推商品或活动、并保持店内可用——采光进得来，店员也能看得出去。先看清橱窗的结构（固定玻璃、玻璃门、竖框）、有多少玻璃面正对阳光、玻璃正后方摆的是什么，这些决定了覆盖多少面积、用哪种材料。

## 以单透膜为橱窗的主体

[单透膜](./products/ys-one-way-vision/)是一种带孔的自粘膜材：从店外看是完整的印刷画面，从店内透过孔眼仍能望出去，也保留采光，适合承担橱窗的大幅活动画面。规划时有两点特性要先知道：

- 效果取决于内外光比。白天室外亮，画面清晰；夜间店内开灯、街道变暗后，单透效果会减弱，店内会变得更可见——夜间的呈现预期要提前想好。
- 孔型是在画面密度与透视清晰度之间取舍：开孔多，透视好；可印面积多，色彩更饱满。如何选要看观看距离和店内向外看的需求，按项目确认。

需要在近处看清的元素——标志、价格、营业时间——通常更适合用[实心背胶](./products/ys-adhesive-vinyl/)贴或刻字贴，与单透膜搭配使用，而不是印在带孔区域上。

## 制作前核对

每一面玻璃单独测量，连同边框与竖框宽度一起记录，并标明哪些是门扇或可开启部分。再确认覆盖是整面还是条带、画面如何跨竖框对位，以及商场或租赁条款对橱窗覆盖是否有规定。玻璃表面状况与清洁处理也要提前确认，膜材的贴合效果很大程度取决于玻璃是否干净完好。

## 规划重点

好的橱窗方案既是画面问题，也是采光问题：单透膜承担活动画面，同时让店内保持明亮、能向外看；实心贴承担精细信息。制作前把玻璃分面、覆盖范围和画面对位确认好，换季时就只是换膜，而不是重新装修。
