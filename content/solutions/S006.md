  ## Start with where the banner hangs and for how long

  Outdoor banners are specified by their placement. Record for each position: what the banner fixes to (fence, frame, building face, poles), the height and viewing distance, how exposed it is to wind and sun, and how long it stays up — a weekend promotion and a season-long facade are different products even at the same size. List repeat locations separately, because standardising sizes across sites that re-order is where most of the saving sits.

  ## Choose weight class and finishing to match the exposure

  [Printed PVC banner](./products/ys-pvc-banner/) is graded by weight, and the common market classes divide the work roughly like this:

  - Lighter classes (around 340gsm in common market terms) suit short campaigns, indoor-outdoor crossover use, and banners that are carried and re-hung often.
  - Mid to heavier classes (around 440–510gsm) suit longer outdoor postings, larger faces, and sites where the banner stays up through weather.
  - Finishing does as much work as the material: reinforced hems spread the load, eyelets at sensible intervals share fixing points, and pole pockets suit frame and scaffold systems. Very large, wind-exposed faces are often better switched to mesh, as covered in the [construction fencing solution](./articles/construction-site-fencing-graphics/).
  - Rigid short-term boards and window or wall pieces near the banner are often better handled by [outdoor PVC film](./products/ys-outdoor-pvc-rigid-film/) and panels planned alongside it, so the campaign reads as one set.

  The weight class and finishing specification for each position are confirmed in the project quotation against size, fixing, and posting period — not chosen from a generic list.

  ## Check details before production

  Confirm the finished size and the fixing points together: eyelet positions are only right if the frame, fence, or wall behind them matches, so measure the structure, not just the space. Check artwork at reading distance — headline text should be sized for the actual distance and speed of the audience, with detail kept out of hems and pocket areas. For Australian campaigns shipped from China, work backwards from the posting date: sea freight to main Australian ports typically takes around 40 days in transit, plus the production time confirmed in your quotation.

  ## Planning focus

  An outdoor banner is a small structure, not just a print: match the weight class to the posting period, let the finishing carry the wind load, and size the message for its real reading distance. Confirm size, fixing, and finishing position by position, and banners go up once and stay readable for the whole campaign.

## 先看挂在哪里、挂多久

户外横幅的规格由投放位置决定。逐个位置记录：固定在什么上面（围栏、框架、建筑立面、立杆）、离地高度与观看距离、迎风和日晒程度、悬挂时长——周末促销和挂一整季的立面横幅，即使尺寸相同，也是两种不同的产品。会重复投放的位置单独列出，因为把常复订的尺寸标准化，是这个品类最实际的省钱点。

## 按暴露程度选克重等级与后道

[印刷 PVC 横幅](./products/ys-pvc-banner/)按克重分级，市场常见等级大致这样分工：

- 较轻等级（市场常见的 340 克左右）适合短期活动、室内外兼用，以及需要经常携带、反复悬挂的横幅。
- 中高等级（市场常见的 440–510 克）适合较长的户外投放、较大的幅面，以及要经受风雨挂满整个周期的位置。
- 后道和材料同样重要：加固包边分散受力，扣眼按合理间距分担固定点，穿绳杆套适配框架与脚手架系统。幅面特别大又迎风的位置，往往更适合改用网布，见[建筑围挡方案](./articles/construction-site-fencing-graphics/)。
- 横幅旁边的短期硬质牌面、贴墙贴窗部件，可与[户外 PVC 膜](./products/ys-outdoor-pvc-rigid-film/)等材料一并规划，让整场活动看起来是一套。

每个位置的克重等级与后道做法，应结合尺寸、固定方式和悬挂周期在项目报价中确认，而不是从一张通用清单里挑。

## 制作前核对

成品尺寸和固定点要一起确认：扣眼位置只有对上身后的框架、围栏或墙面才算对，所以要量结构本身，不只是量空间。再按真实阅读距离检查稿面——标题字的大小要匹配受众的距离与移动速度，细节内容避开包边和杆套区域。

## 规划重点

户外横幅是个小结构，不只是一张印刷品：克重等级对着悬挂周期选，后道负责扛住风载，信息大小对着真实阅读距离定。逐个位置把尺寸、固定和后道确认好，横幅一次挂好、整个投放期都清楚可读。
