Choosing large-format print media starts with the job, not simply the lowest price or highest gsm. Consider the display environment, surface appearance, mounting method, roll dimensions and any exposure requirements before comparing products.

## 1. Gloss and satin photo paper: for poster and indoor display work

Gloss photo paper provides a brighter, reflective surface appearance. Satin photo paper offers a softer surface appearance. Both are supplied in 1520 mm roll width, 200 gsm media weight and 50 m rolls.

These are useful starting points for posters, promotional graphics, retail displays and indoor advertising. Choose the surface finish according to the look you want and the lighting in the display area.

## 2. PP synthetic paper: check the exposure requirements

The listed PP synthetic paper is 1520 mm wide, 180 gsm and supplied in 50 m rolls. It can be considered for posters, promotional graphics and display projects.

The product name includes “outdoor”, but a factory weather-resistance rating or long-term outdoor service-life test has not been supplied. Do not assume a guaranteed outdoor lifetime from the name alone. Confirm the intended exposure, ink system and finishing with the supplier before specifying it for a long-term outdoor installation.

## 3. Self-adhesive vinyl: specify the liner and confirm adhesive details

Self-adhesive vinyl is suited to graphics that need an adhesive mounting method, such as signs, retail graphics and applied displays. The listed roll width is 1520 mm and the roll length is 50 m.

Two release-liner weights are offered: 120 gsm and 140 gsm. These figures describe the backing liner, not the thickness or gsm of the printable face film. The adhesive colour, adhesive type, permanence and face-film thickness have not been confirmed and should be checked with the supplier before ordering.

## 4. Outdoor PVC printing film: waterproof matte-coated PVC

The listed PVC printing film uses a PVC base with a waterproof matte coating. Confirmed specifications are 1270 mm roll width, 240 gsm and 50 m per roll. Waterproof and oxidation-resistant properties have been confirmed, but no quantified service-life or weathering test rating is stated here.

It is intended for outdoor print and display projects where the specified PVC material and matte surface are suitable. Confirm the installation conditions and any additional finishing before ordering.

## 5. Confirm the complete print configuration

The standard ink option is Epson-compatible replacement ink; original Epson ink is also available as an option, with pricing confirmed per quotation. Standard finishing includes 100 gsm PVC cold-lamination film with a yellow release liner, with gloss or matte finish options. These are configuration details, not a substitute for confirming compatibility with the actual media and intended environment.

Before requesting a quotation, provide the product, roll width, quantity or area, destination, ink choice, lamination preference and packing requirements. The minimum order quantity is confirmed against the actual specification of each order, so please state your required area and specification when enquiring.

## Quick selection checklist

- Choose photo paper when the required result is a glossy or satin poster/display surface.
- Consider PP synthetic paper for poster and promotional work, but verify outdoor exposure requirements.
- Choose self-adhesive vinyl when the graphic needs an adhesive mounting method; specify the liner weight and confirm adhesive details before ordering.
- Consider the PVC printing film when its 1270 mm width, 240 gsm specification and waterproof matte-coated PVC construction fit the project.

If the application is unusual or the installation is exposed to demanding conditions, share the details before ordering so the material can be checked against the actual use.
