HOW ORDERING WORKS

The same process, whatever the order.

01

Enquiry

Send the product, application, dimensions, quantity and the Australian delivery location.

02

Quotation & proforma invoice

Specifications, packing, freight and delivery terms are confirmed in writing in your quotation and proforma invoice, before you commit to anything.

03

Payment

Payment is by bank transfer (T/T) against the proforma invoice.

04

File check & production

Your artwork is checked personally; anything that could affect the result is flagged back first, then the order is produced with the partner facility matched to the job.

05

QC photos, export packing & shipment

You receive photos of the finished goods and the packing for confirmation before dispatch. Goods are packed for export and shipped on the terms in your quotation — sea freight to main Australian ports typically takes around 40 days in transit, and any import charges for your destination are stated in the quotation.

FREIGHT, CONFIRMED PER ORDER

No generic freight promises. Your quotation is the promise.

Every order is quoted individually, because the right freight arrangement depends on what you are ordering and where it is going. Sea freight to main Australian ports typically takes around 40 days in transit and is arranged per order — the total lead time (production plus transit) and delivery terms are confirmed in your quotation before you commit. If a detail cannot be confirmed, you will be told that plainly rather than given a guess.

To get an accurate quotation, it helps to include:

  • The product or media type, and what it will be used for
  • Finished dimensions, or the roll size you need
  • Quantity, and whether the order is one-off or repeating
  • Whether artwork is print-ready or still needs checking
  • The delivery location in Australia
